# SkillBridge – Social Impact & Vision

## 🌟 Vision Statement

**"Every skill deserves recognition. Every worker deserves opportunity."**

SkillBridge envisions a future where economic opportunity is not limited by language, literacy, or formal education—where a vegetable vendor's inventory management skills are recognized as valuable as an MBA graduate's, and where a homemaker's multitasking abilities open doors to formal employment.

## 🇮🇳 Alignment with National Priorities

### Skill India Mission
- **NSQF Integration**: Map informal skills to National Skills Qualifications Framework
- **PMKVY Linkage**: Connect users to Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana training
- **Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)**: Validate existing skills without formal training

### Digital India
- **Digital Inclusion**: Voice-first interface for low digital literacy users
- **Aadhaar Integration**: Secure identity verification
- **DigiLocker**: Store resumes and certificates digitally

### Atmanirbhar Bharat
- **Local Language First**: Prioritize Indian languages over English
- **Domestic Employment**: Keep talent within India
- **MSME Support**: Connect workers to small businesses

### Financial Inclusion
- **Jan Dhan Integration**: Link to bank accounts for salary deposits
- **Credit Score Building**: Formal employment history for loan eligibility
- **Insurance Access**: Enable PMJJBY and PMSBY enrollment

## 💰 Business Model & Sustainability

### Phase 1: Free for Workers (Always)
- No cost to job seekers
- Funded by grants and CSR

### Phase 2: Employer Subscriptions
- ₹999/month: Access to candidate database
- ₹4,999/month: Premium features (verified candidates, analytics)
- ₹19,999/month: Enterprise (API access, bulk hiring)

### Phase 3: Government Partnerships
- Integration fees from state employment departments
- Training program partnerships (NSDC, PMKVY)
- Data analytics for policy making

### Phase 4: Value-Added Services
- Skill verification tests (₹99 per test)
- Professional resume printing (₹50 per resume)
- Interview preparation (₹499 per course)
- Micro-loans for skill training (commission-based)

**Projected Revenue (Year 3):**
- Employer subscriptions: ₹50 crore
- Government partnerships: ₹20 crore
- Value-added services: ₹10 crore
- **Total: ₹80 crore annually**
